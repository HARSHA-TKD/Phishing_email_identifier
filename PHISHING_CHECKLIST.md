# Phishing Email Detection Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing an unexpected or suspicious email.

## 1. Sender Verification
- [ ] Does the sender address match the organisation's real domain?
- [ ] Is the display name different from the actual email address?
- [ ] Are there unusual spelling changes in the domain?
- [ ] Was the email expected?

## 2. Subject and Message
- [ ] Does the email create pressure or demand immediate action?
- [ ] Does it threaten account suspension or closure?
- [ ] Does it promise an unexpected prize or reward?
- [ ] Does it contain unusual spelling or grammar?
- [ ] Does the request seem out of character?

## 3. Links and Attachments
- [ ] Does the visible link destination match the claimed organisation?
- [ ] Is the URL shortened or unusually formatted?
- [ ] Does the URL use an unfamiliar or misspelled domain?
- [ ] Was the attachment expected and verified?
- [ ] Is the message asking you to open an unexpected file?

Do not open suspicious links or attachments just to inspect them.

## 4. Sensitive Information
- [ ] Does the email request a password, OTP, or security code?
- [ ] Does it ask for banking or payment information?
- [ ] Does it ask you to sign in through an unexpected link?

Never share passwords or OTPs in response to an email.

## 5. Verification and Action
- [ ] Have I verified the request through an independent trusted channel?
- [ ] Have I avoided clicking suspicious links?
- [ ] Have I reported the message through the appropriate channel?

## Final Assessment

- **Likely suspicious:** Multiple warning signs are present.
- **Needs verification:** One or more warning signs require investigation.
- **Insufficient evidence:** No obvious warning signs, but safety is not guaranteed.

Remember: This checklist supports investigation. It cannot prove that an email is safe or malicious.
